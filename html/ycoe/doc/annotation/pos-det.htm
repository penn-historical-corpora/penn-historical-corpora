The following words are tagged D: 

        A, AN, THE, THIS, THAT, THESE, THOSE, YON, YONDER
Demonstratives are always tagged D, with or without a head noun. 
        and_CONJ cristened_VBD hym_PRO at_P +te_D citee_N 
        Dortik_NPR ,_, +tat_D is_BEP Dorchestre_NPR

        +tat_D is_BEP Friday_NPR

        +Tis_D +tat_C is_BEP i-seide_VAN in_P +te_D comyn_ADJ table_N

        For_FOR to_TO brynge_VB +tis_D aboute_RP Machometus_NPR 
        norsched_VBD and_CONJ fedde_VBD a_D faire_ADJ camel_N

AN (Determiner) or ONE (Number)

 In cases ambiguous between ONE and AN (i.e., ONE or D), the default is to tag it as a determiner.

YONDER

 YONDER is tagged D when it modifies a noun, but ADV when used adverbially.
