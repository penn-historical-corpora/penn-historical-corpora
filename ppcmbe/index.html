PPCMBE

Penn Parsed Corpus of Modern British English, 2nd edition, release 1

Citation

Kroch, Anthony, Beatrice Santorini, and C.E.A. Diertani. 2016. The Penn Parsed Corpus of Modern British English (PPCMBE2). Department of Linguistics, University of Pennsylvania. Currently distributed by the Linguistic Data Consortium as part of the Penn Parsed Corpora of Historical English.
