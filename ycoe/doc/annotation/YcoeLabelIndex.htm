In the index, labels in parentheses following the main entry indicate labels that may cooccur with the main entry. For instance,
  • (^N, ^A, ^G, ^D, ^I) following a part-of-speech entry indicates that the label may be followed by one of the listed case labels
  • function labels (-PRN, -SPE) following a phrase entry indicates that the label may be followed by one or more of the listed labels
  • the phrase case labels listed following an entry (-NOM, -ACC, -GEN, -DAT), if present, always follow the phrase label immediately, coming before any function labels; thus NP-PRD (-NOM, -ACC, -GEN) represents NP-PRD, NP-NOM-PRD, NOM-ACC-PRD, and NP-GEN-PRD
  • a label with -CASE included in the label, as in PTP-CASE-ABS, indicates that a case label is required; that is, PTP-ABS does not occur
Labels may also be followed by an index number attached to the label with either a hyphen (e.g., NP-ACC-1, IP-MAT-0) or in the case of incomplete clauses (IPX-, CPX-, PTPX-) with an equal sign (e.g., IPX-MAT=0, IPX-SUB-PRN=0)
