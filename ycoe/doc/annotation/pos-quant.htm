Quantified nouns

 Quantified nouns are treated as combinations of Q plus THING, ONE, PLACE, WIHT, etc. 
        something_Q+N           someone_Q+ONE           someplace_Q+N

Comparative and superlative quantifiers

 FEWER and FEWEST are always tagged QR and QS respectively. MORE, LESS, and MOST, LEAST can be quantifiers, or adjectives (in the meaning GREATER, GREATEST, and SMALLER, SMALLEST). Although it is not possible to distinguish all the cases, we tag them as adjectives in the following clear contexts and as quantifiers in all other cases. Genitive/Possessive quantifiers

 Although genitive/possessive forms of quantifiers are not normally marked, they are marked in the following cases:
  1. when no noun is present in a possessive NP to carry the possessive marker 
           ure_PRO$ alre_Q$ fader_N        father of all of us
       here_PRO$ beire_Q$ luue_N       love of both of them
  2. in essentially superlative adjectival and adverbial expressions like ALRE FIRST (FIRST OF ALL) and ALRE BEST (BEST OF ALL), etc. 
           alre_Q$ sorest_ADVS               sorest of all
       alre_Q$ fairest_ADJS              fairest of all
       hegest_ADJS alre_Q$ lor+tew_N     highest teacher of all
See also Section POSSESSIVES AND GENITIVES.
