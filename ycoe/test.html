<h3 ALIGN=CENTER><A href="YcoeConditions.htm">Conditions of Use</A></H4>
<p>
<HR>

The York-Toronto-Helsinki Parsed Corpus of Old English Prose (YCOE) is a
1.5 million word syntactically-annotated corpus. As a sister corpus to the
Penn-Helsinki Parsed Corpus of Middle English (<a
href="http://www.ling.upenn.edu/mideng/ppcme2dir">PPCME2</a>), it uses the
same form of annotation and is accessed by the same search engine,
CorpusSearch.  CorpusSearch is not included in this distribution. If you
already own CorpusSearch you can search the YCOE with it; if not,
instructions for obtaining it are given below.
<p>
